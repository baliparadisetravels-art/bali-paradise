Terms & Conditions

Simple, fair rules so we're all on the same page.

The short version: Cancel 24+ hours ahead = totally free. Follow your guide's instructions = no damage charges ever. We're fair and upfront. No tricks, no hidden fees.

1. Booking

  • Your booking is confirmed once we reply on WhatsApp or email with a date and time.
  • Most tours: no upfront payment needed. You pay on the day.
  • All prices are in Indonesian Rupiah (IDR).
  • You must be 18+ to book. Kids are welcome with a parent/guardian.
  • If you have a medical condition or allergy, please tell us when you book.

2. What's Included

  • Hotel pickup and drop-off
  • English-speaking guide
  • All equipment and safety gear
  • Basic activity insurance
  • Drinking water

Not included: meals (unless stated on the tour page), personal spending, tips, extra activities you add on the day.

3. Cancellation

When You Cancel What Happens
24+ hours before the tour 100% free. No charge.
12-24 hours before 50% fee
Less than 12 hours Full price charged
No-show (you don't turn up, no message) Full price + any costs we already paid out

Why? When you book, we lock in a guide, car, and gear just for you. If you cancel last-minute, we can't fill that spot and we've already paid our suppliers.

If WE cancel (bad weather, volcano alert, government closure), you get your choice of:

  • Reschedule for free
  • Full refund
  • Credit towards another tour

4. Pickup

  • We confirm your pickup time the evening before via WhatsApp.
  • Please be in your hotel lobby on time.
  • We'll wait 15 minutes, then call you. No reply after another 10 min = no-show.
  • If you're running late but message us, we'll wait up to 30 minutes.

If we're late: 30+ minutes late = 10% discount. 60+ minutes = you can cancel free.

5. Equipment & Damage

Normal wear and tear? Never your problem. We only charge you if something is deliberately damaged or you ignore safety rules.

What Happened You Pay
Normal use, stuff gets scratched/worn Nothing
Broke something by being reckless Repair cost (no markup)
Lost gear (helmet, torch, etc.) Replacement cost
ATV damage from going off-track Up to IDR 5,000,000
ATV totalled (flipped, drowned) Full replacement value

Bottom line: Follow your guide, stay on the track, and you'll never owe a cent for damage. This only kicks in if you deliberately ignore safety rules.

Maximum charge for any single incident: IDR 25,000,000. We always show you photo evidence before asking for payment.

6. Behaviour

Our guide can end the tour immediately (no refund) if you:

  • Are drunk or high during an adventure activity
  • Ignore safety warnings after being told twice
  • Are aggressive towards anyone
  • Damage sacred or cultural sites

7. Safety & Fitness

Tour Fitness Needed
Sunrise Trek Moderate (2hr uphill walk)
Rafting Moderate (must be able to swim)
ATV Moderate (need upper body strength)
Cultural Tours Easy (walking on uneven ground)

Get your own travel insurance. We include basic activity cover, but it won't cover your phone, missed flights, or pre-existing conditions.

8. Payment

  • Cash IDR to your guide on the day (preferred)
  • Bank transfer (BCA/BRI) — details on request
  • Foreign cash accepted at the day's Google rate
  • No credit cards directly (sorry!)
  • Groups of 5+ or multi-day tours: 30% deposit may be needed

9. Respect the Culture

  • Temples: Wear a sarong (we provide them). Don't climb on structures.
  • Offerings: Those little baskets on the ground (canang sari) are sacred. Step around them.
  • Nature: Don't litter. Don't feed or harass monkeys.
  • Temple entry: Women who are menstruating cannot enter (this is Balinese religious custom).

10. Your Data

  • We only use your contact info to manage your booking.
  • We never sell your details.
  • We might use anonymous tour photos on our socials. If your face is visible, we'll ask first.

11. If Something Goes Wrong

  • Step 1: Message us on WhatsApp. Most things get sorted in a day.
  • Step 2: If still unhappy, we sit down and talk it through (musyawarah — the Indonesian way).
  • Step 3: If that fails, either party can go to BPSK (Consumer Dispute Board) in Gianyar, Bali.

These terms follow Indonesian law.
