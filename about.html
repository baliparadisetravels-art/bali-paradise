Meet Your Guide

Hi, I'm Kadek Wan

I was born and raised in Bali, and this island is my home, my culture, and my passion. I started guiding tourists because I wanted to share the Bali that guidebooks don't show — the hidden waterfalls, the secret temple ceremonies, the family-run coffee farms where they still roast beans by hand.

What began as showing friends around has grown into a full-time adventure company. But my philosophy hasn't changed: every tour is personal, every guest is a friend, and every day in Bali should feel magical.

Fully insured and fluent in English, I've spent years sharing Bali's most breathtaking landscapes with guests from all over the world. Whether you want heart-pumping adventure or peaceful cultural immersion, I'll design the perfect Bali experience for you.
